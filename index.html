Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about admissions, batches, demo classes, and fee structure.

Which classes and boards do you teach?

We provide comprehensive academic coaching for students from Class 6 to Class 12 across both CBSE and UP Board curricula. We teach in Hindi, English, and Hinglish so every student understands concepts thoroughly.

Can my child attend a free demo class before admission?

Yes, absolutely! We offer a 2-day complimentary diagnostic demo class for all new students. Parents are welcome to visit our classrooms, inspect study materials, and meet the faculty before finalizing admission.

What are the batch timings at BIDCG Coaching Centre?

We operate two convenient shifts to fit school schedules:
• Morning Batch: 5:00 AM to 7:00 AM (Ideal for board revision & self-study)
• Afternoon / Evening Batch: 2:00 PM to 9:00 PM (Divided by classes 6 to 12)

How are students' doubts cleared?

We maintain a dedicated Doubt Clearing Desk available daily after every class. Students can sit 1-on-1 with teachers to resolve homework questions, board exemplar problems, or unclear concepts.

How do parents track student progress and attendance?

We send instant attendance alerts via SMS/WhatsApp if a student is absent. Sunday test answer sheets and marks are shared with parents, followed by periodic Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs).

What is the fee payment structure? Is there a sibling discount?

Fees can be paid monthly or quarterly via Cash, UPI, or Bank Transfer, with printed fee receipts. We also offer a 10% sibling concession when two children from the same family enroll together.
